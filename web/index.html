A Eurorack frame is two side panels, one folded body, and two rails per row bolted between the panels with an M5 at each end. Everything below the width is derived from the format.

A hybrid case is a laser-cut Eurorack frame lying flat, with parts sections above and below it. Its width in HP is the width of the whole case; the deck top is the module face, and the flat surface your boards are flush with.

parts sections

Stretches of deck above and below the rack, as wide as the rails. Each has its own length and its own depth under the deck; place boards in them from the parts palette. Several on one side stack outwards in list order. A part outside its section, in the rack's space or deeper than its floor is an error.

How far each side panel reaches past the plates: bottom and top past the end walls, front past the deck, back past the floors. With T-slots it is at least a sheet everywhere.

The rail stands 2.7 mm proud of the body. That is where the extrusion's sheet slot is, not a choice — there is one rail profile and the body has to meet it.

How far each panel edge reaches past the case. Bottom and top decide what it rests on: below 2.7 mm the rails carry it, above that the panel does. Front and back are pure lip.
